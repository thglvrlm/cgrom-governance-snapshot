CGROM Governance Snapshot, version 2.0

How governed is your cyber risk?

For U.S. regulated companies and the vendors that serve them. Fifteen simple statements across five pillars: in about five minutes you get a basic view of your cyber governance maturity, a score for each pillar, where to start and the U.S. rules linked to your gaps.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Your answers stay in your browser
  • Based on the CGROM Open Edition v2.0 (CC BY 4.0)
  1. Score 15 statementsThree per pillar, on a 0 to 3 scale.
  2. See your profileAn overall level and a score for each pillar.
  3. Plan the next stepWhere to start, plus the U.S. rules linked to each gap.

How to score each statement

Answer for the organization as it operates today, not as it intends to operate. If a practice covers only part of the company, score the critical part. If two people disagree, record the lower score: the disagreement is itself a finding.

    About this assessment

    Method
    The 15 core statements, scoring and five-level maturity model come from the CGROM Open Edition v2.0, Section 15 (the core statements are unchanged from v1.0, so results stay comparable). Each pillar score is the sum of its three answers divided by 9; the overall result is the average of the five pillars.

    Privacy
    Your individual answers are processed only in your browser and are never sent. When you view your result, an anonymous count of the completed assessment (overall level, percentage and language, with no name or contact details) is recorded through our hosting provider, Netlify, so we know how many assessments are completed.
    Limits
    This Snapshot gives a basic, directional view of maturity. It is not an audit, a certification or legal advice.
