How governed is your cyber risk?

Fifteen statements across five pillars. In about five minutes you get a maturity level, a pillar-by-pillar profile, the first improvements to make and the U.S. framework requirements linked to your gaps.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Answers stay in your browser
  • Based on the CGROM Open Edition (CC BY 4.0)
  1. Score 15 statementsThree per pillar, on a 0 to 3 scale.
  2. See your profileAn overall level and a score for each pillar.
  3. Plan the next stepPriorities, plus the U.S. requirements linked to each gap.

How to score each statement

    About this assessment

    Method
    The statements, scoring and five-level maturity model come from the CGROM Open Edition, Section 8. Each pillar score is the sum of its three answers divided by 9; the overall result is the average of the five pillars.
    Source
    CGROM Open Edition by Thiago Oliveira Lima, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Version 1.0, DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.33963217, and version 2.0, DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.34045599. The fifteen statements are identical in both editions, so results stay comparable. doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33963217
    Privacy
    Your answers are processed only in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere, and closing the page erases them.
    Limits
    This Snapshot gives a directional view. It is not an audit, a certification or legal advice.
